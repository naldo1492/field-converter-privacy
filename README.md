# Field Converter — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Field Converter does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

- The app works entirely on your device and does not connect to the internet.
- Your settings, saved conversions, and history are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.
- The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.
- When you tap Copy, the result is placed on your device's clipboard at your request.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to delete or request from us.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/naldo1492/field-converter-privacy/issues
